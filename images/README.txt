IMAGE GUIDELINES:
================

1. Use high-quality, professional photos
2. Compress images for web (use TinyPNG or similar)
3. Maintain aspect ratios
4. Use JPG for photos, PNG for logos/graphics
5. Ensure photos are well-lit and clear
6. Get permission for photos of people
7. Avoid copyrighted images

PLACEHOLDER IMAGES:
==================

Until you have actual photos, you can use:
- Placeholder.com
- Unsplash.com (free stock photos)
- Pexels.com (free stock photos)

Or create colored placeholders with text using any image editor.

PHOTO SOURCES:
=============

1. Village Photography: Take photos with smartphone/camera
2. Aerial Views: Use drone or Google Earth screenshots
3. Officials: Professional portraits
4. Historical: Scan old photographs
5. Religious Sites: Visit and photograph

IMPORTANT:
==========

- Do not use copyrighted images without permission
- Ensure all people in photos have given consent
- Maintain dignity and respect in all imagery
- Follow government guidelines for official photos
