To add images:

1. Create these folders:
   - images/slider/
   - images/admin/
   - images/sites/

2. Add any photos with these names:
   - images/emblem.png
   - images/slider/gomti-river.jpg
   - images/slider/village-fields.jpg
   - images/slider/market.jpg
   - images/slider/temple.jpg
   - images/slider/aerial-view.jpg

Or use free stock photos from:
- unsplash.com
- pexels.com
- pixabay.com

The website works perfectly without images too - they just show as broken image icons.
